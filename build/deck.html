Problem Statement
Design a Case Management Workbench MVP (web) for a health plan that (a) assembles a timely, trustworthy, holistic picture of each member; (b) tells the case manager whom to work on and what to do next; and (c) cuts documentation and outreach friction, so clinicians spend their time on judgment and conversations, not data hunting.
